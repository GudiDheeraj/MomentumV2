Why not just use a job board

Most job trackers only watch the last mile.

A kanban board of "Applied / Interview / Offer" tells you where a role stands — but it has no idea whether you studied for the interview, kept up the habit of applying daily, or tailored the resume that got you the callback.

Momentum Tracker treats the job search as what it actually is: one output of a bigger system of habits, tasks, and study time. Everything feeds the same board, the same score, the same streak.
